# MITS SubTracker — User Guide

A quick guide to everything the app does. (This same content is available inside
the app via the **? Help** button in the top bar.)

## The three tabs

- **Dashboard** — your at-a-glance overview: monthly & yearly totals, what's
  renewing soon, spend by category, spend by owner, and a 12-month spend chart.
  This view spans *everything* — your subscriptions and all clients'.
- **My Subs** — just your personal subscriptions, with search, filters, sort,
  and bulk actions.
- **Clients** — your client roster. Open a client to see and manage their
  subscriptions with their own mini-dashboard.

## Adding & editing a subscription

Click **+ Add** (top right). Fields:

- **Type** — *Subscription* (recurring), *Lifetime* (one-time purchase),
  *Trial*, or *Revenue* (money coming in).
- **Owner** — *Me (Personal)* or a client. Adding from inside a client defaults
  to that client.
- **Amount + Currency**, **Billing Cycle** (weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly).
- **Recurring** toggle — off = a one-time charge.
- **Category, Start/Next billing date, Website, Payment method, Tags, Notes.**
- **Status** — Active or Paused (paused subs don't count toward totals).

**Lifetime** purchases can include a *Comparable Monthly Plan* price — the app
then shows when the purchase "pays for itself" and your effective cost per
month/day.

## Card actions

Each subscription card has: **Edit**, **Pause/Resume**, **Duplicate**, and
**Delete**, plus **View payment history** (a computed billing history with total
paid to date).

## Bulk actions (My Subs)

Click **Select**, tick the subscriptions you want, then **Pause**, **Resume**,
**Add Tag**, or **Delete** them all at once.

## Clients

In **Clients → Add Client**, enter name, company, email, phone, notes. Open a
client to view their dashboard and subscriptions. Deleting a client also deletes
their subscriptions.

## Alerts (🔔)

Click the bell → enable alerts, choose how many days before a renewal to be
warned (1/3/7/14/30), and optionally turn on **browser/desktop notifications**
and **Launch at login** (so reminders fire in the background via the tray).

## Currency (⚙ Settings)

Set your **base currency** — all dashboard totals convert to it. Individual
subscriptions keep their own currency. Adjust exchange rates if needed.

## Backup & restore (⚙ Settings)

- **Export full backup (JSON)** — everything; keep these safe.
- **Export subscriptions (CSV)** — spreadsheet-friendly.
- **Import from backup (JSON)** — restores from a backup (replaces current data).

> 💡 Your data lives only on this computer. Export a backup periodically.

## Tray & updates

Closing the window **minimizes to the system tray** (it keeps running so alerts
work) — right-click the tray icon → **Quit** to fully exit. When a new version
is available, a banner offers **Install & Restart**; you can also check manually
in **Settings → Check for updates**.
